## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the sensory prestige of luxury perfumery fused with the surgical precision of enterprise resource planning. Built for multi-brand cosmetic and fragrance curators managing high-tier portfolios (such as O Boticário, Eudora, and O.U.i), the UI balances operational high-density data management with the tactility and poise of a boutique atelier.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Direction
The movement is **Curated Warm Minimalism with Architectural Skeuomorphic Whispers**:
- **Atmospheric Palette**: Luminous warm parchment bases replace sterile white canvas, anchored by deep graphite structures, warm amber infusions, and velvety grand-cru wine accents.
- **Data Poise**: Complex inventory metrics, batch tracking, and multi-channel cashflow models are presented not as clinical spreadsheets, but as curated financial ledgers and product folios.
- **Emotional Resonance**: Evokes discretion, tactile luxury, bespoke craftsmanship, and effortless institutional command.

## Colors

The color system reconciles high-contrast operational readability with artisanal fragrance house aesthetics. It balances warm mineral neutrals against rich pigment accents.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#7A1C30` — Vinho Nobre)**: Used for primary high-intent action buttons, authoritative metrics, active selection states, and brand-defining anchor accents.
- **Secondary (`#C59341` — Âmbar Dourado)**: Highlights luxury statuses, batch maturation badges, high-margin revenue indicators, alerts requiring discerning attention, and gilded graphical accents.
- **Tertiary (`#D8A499` — Rosé Suave)**: Delicate informational fills, micro-interactions, subtle highlight badges, and promotional channel tags.
- **Neutral (`#1C1B1A` — Grafite Profundo)**: High-contrast typographical grounding, sidebar frames, dark table headers, and structural divides.
- **Base Canvas & Surfaces**:
  - `surface-canvas`: `#FAF8F5` (Parchment White)
  - `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF`
  - `surface-subtle`: `#F3EFEA` (Warm alabaster for table headers, zebra-striping, and secondary toolbars)
  - `border-hairline`: `rgba(28, 27, 26, 0.08)`

## Typography

The typographical pairing expresses dual authority: the editorial elegance of luxury lifestyle publishing coupled with geometric ergonomics for dense information grids.

- **Editorial Headings (`Noto Serif`)**: Brings high-end perfume packaging sophistication to dashboards, brand showcase sections, executive KPIs, and ledger headers.
- **Interface & Operational Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Clean, humanistic geometry optimized for legible inventory tables, SKU tracking, financial journals, and checkout funnels.
- **Tabular Numerics**: Apply `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1` across all currency, stock, and batch tables to guarantee monospaced alignment across high-frequency transactional displays.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a hybrid **Fixed-Sidebar + 12-Column Fluid Data Canvas**, tailored for desktop management suites while degrading gracefully to tablet and mobile order-taking terminals.

### Architecture
- **Desktop (>=1280px)**: A fixed 260px left navigation rail (collapsible to 72px icon mode) paired with a 12-column dynamic grid. Column gutters scale at `gutter-desktop` (1.5rem), and outer canvas margins anchor at `margin-desktop` (2rem). High-density metric panels utilize compact `space-md` gaps.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: A 6-to-8 column adaptive layout with a drawer navigation system, prioritizing unified cashflow bars and batch status lists.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: A single-column vertical flow with sticky bottom action trays for quick POS inventory scans and cash register operations.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Tonal Layering combined with Low-Contrast Hairline Outlines and Warm Ambient Radiance**. High-intensity drop shadows are discarded in favor of refined, luminous depth.

- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas)**: `#FAF8F5`. Ground level for main application viewports.
- **Level 1 (Data Cards & Workstations)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` resting on `#FAF8F5`, delineated by a 1px border of `rgba(28, 27, 26, 0.06)` and an ultra-diffuse ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(28, 27, 26, 0.03), 0 6px 16px rgba(122, 28, 48, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Flyouts & Filter Panels)**: `#FFFFFF` paired with an elevated warm blur: `0 8px 24px rgba(28, 27, 26, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(197, 147, 65, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Critical Reconciliations)**: Supported by a warm graphite backdrop scrim (`rgba(28, 27, 26, 0.45)` with 4px backdrop blur) and a structured shadow: `0 20px 48px rgba(28, 27, 26, 0.16)`.
- **Gilded Focus Accent**: Focused interactive controls feature an inner 1px border with an outer ring glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 147, 65, 0.20)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a **Soft Architectural (`roundedness: 1`)** geometry. 

- Form controls, high-density table cells, and standard buttons utilize a subtle `4px` (`0.25rem`) radius, reflecting the crisp lines of luxury perfume flacons and gift boxes.
- Floating metric modules and elevated analytical cards adopt `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- Strict exception: Badges and micro category tags utilize full pill contours (`9999px`) to create distinction between functional inputs and passive metadata.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep wine base (`#7A1C30`), crisp white label (`#FFFFFF`), 4px radius, 36px height (compact desktop density). Subtle hover transition to `#5E1524`.
- **Secondary / Gilded**: Alabaster fill (`#FAF8F5`), 1px amber-hairline border (`rgba(197, 147, 65, 0.5)`), graphite text (`#1C1B1A`). Hover shifts border to solid `#C59341` with a tinted glow.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Borderless, quiet graphite typography, shifting to `#7A1C30` with a subtle rosé background wash (`rgba(216, 164, 153, 0.12)`) on hover.

### Data Tables & High-Density Grids
- **Header Rows**: Uppercase tracking via `label-sm`, set against `#F3EFEA` with a bottom border of `rgba(28, 27, 26, 0.1)`.
- **Rows**: 44px compact row height for desktop inventory monitoring. Hover state applies an amber-tinted wash (`rgba(197, 147, 65, 0.03)`).
- **Cells**: Strict vertical alignment; currency and numerical stock figures formatted via `numeric-table` tokens.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Brand Identifiers**:
  - *O Boticário*: Subtle jade/emerald wash (`#EBF5F0` fill with `#1B6B48` text).
  - *Eudora*: Rich plum tint (`#F6EDF4` fill with `#641D58` text).
  - *O.U.i*: Refined champagne gold (`#F9F5EC` fill with `#8B6A2B` text).
- **Inventory Health**:
  - In Stock: Soft botanical green chip.
  - Low Stock: Âmbar chip (`#FFF8EB` fill, `#946300` text, 1px amber border).
  - Critical / Breakage: Deep wine chip (`#FDF2F4` fill, `#7A1C30` text).

### Form Controls & Financial Inputs
- **Inputs**: 36px height, neutral warm border (`rgba(28, 27, 26, 0.15)`), white surface, 4px radius.
- **Active / Focused**: Border shifts to `#C59341` accompanied by the warm amber atmospheric glow.
- **Financial Prefixes**: Fixed currency symbol (`R$`) rendered in muted graphite inside a `#F3EFEA` container inset.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Custom 16px boxes with 3px inner corner radius. Unchecked borders rest at `rgba(28, 27, 26, 0.2)`. Checked state floods with `#7A1C30` and displays a crisp white checkmark.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Olfactory Note & Batch Ribbon**: Compact metadata ribbon displaying Fragrance Family (Amadeirado, Floral, Oriental), Batch Number, Expiration, and Tax Inscription (NCM/ST).
- **Omnichannel Stock Ledger**: Dual-level progress meters illustrating POS shelf availability vs. E-commerce reserved fulfillment quotas.
- **Cash Flow Reconciliation Drawer**: Slide-over panel featuring ledger debit/credit balances, payment gateway settlement schedules (Pix, Credit, Multi-brand vouchers), and automated markup margins.
